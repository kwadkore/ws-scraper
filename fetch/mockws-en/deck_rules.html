Deck Construction Rules

 

Standard Constructed

All cards that have been officially released can be used.
 

Side Constructed

You may only use cards from either "Weiß Side" or "Schwarz Side" in your deck.
 

Neo-Standard Constructed

You may only include cards from the same title in your deck.
